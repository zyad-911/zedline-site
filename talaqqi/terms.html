Learning aid, not a teacher's ijazah

Talaqqi compares your recitation with a sheikh's and gives automated feedback on melody, timing, breath and words. It is a practice companion. It does not replace learning tajwid and recitation from a qualified teacher, and its scores are guidance, not a certification.
